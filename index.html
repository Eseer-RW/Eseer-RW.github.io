a little about me

i’m a quantitative developer who likes the unglamorous half of the work: clean data, honest backtests, and code that still makes sense six months later. most of what i build starts as a question i couldn’t stop turning over.

before this i studied mathematics and spent a few years closer to research than to production, which is where i picked up the habit of writing things down carefully. i care about tools that are small enough to understand end to end.

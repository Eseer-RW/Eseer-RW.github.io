the path so far

where i’ve been studying and working.

  1. 2025 — 2029

    b.a. mathematics, statistics & psychology · harvard university

    math 55a (group theory and linear algebra), math 55b (real and complex analysis), cs50, cs1240 (data structures and algorithms), and ensc s-138 (probability, statistics and their applications to engineering and data science). cambridge, ma

  2. may — august 2026

    swe intern · insilicom

    built a citation-fabrication detector over 486m locally indexed scholarly works, reaching 95% recall and 94% specificity on the largest public benchmark of confirmed real-world fabrications, with ram-resident 168gb indexes and gil-aware process sharding cutting per-paper verification by ~30×. ran the largest per-citation fabrication study of arxiv to date — 80m+ citations across 2m papers — and produced the first verified fabrication rate for scientific preprints. woburn, ma

  3. may — august 2026

    fellow · center market strategies llc

    authored a research memorandum on systemic risk from ai adoption across insurance, securities and crypto markets, synthesising 45+ sources across federal legislation, regulatory filings, litigation and central bank reports. washington, dc

  4. january — may 2026

    software engineer · harvard computer society, tech for social good

    built a python llm benchmarking tool comparing 7 machine-translation systems (gpt-4.1, gemini 3, claude, nllb-200) on 778k+ chichewa–english sentence pairs for humanitarian-tech client ushahidi, scored with bleu, chrf++, bertscore and comet. cambridge, ma

  5. july 2025

    wise program participant · jane street

    trained in market microstructure and live market-making through quantitative trading simulation workshops and coursework with firm traders. new york, ny
